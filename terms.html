This is the agreement between you and heynewell when you hire us. It's written to be read, not skimmed past. If anything here conflicts with a signed proposal or invoice for your specific job, that document wins for your job.

01 — the services

heynewell is a done-for-you services business operated by Richmond Park Group LLC (referred to here as "we", "us", or "heynewell"). We sell three lanes of fixed-price work:

  • Presence — business email setup and migration ($150 one-time), website design and launch ($900 one-time), and ongoing care ($95/month, cancel anytime).
  • Construction (nationwide) — material takeoffs ($250/trade), plan review ($350), shop drawings (from $90/sheet), submittal and vendor coordination (from $600/month), and site verification visits ($250/visit).
  • AI, finished — finishing work you started with AI tools: Session Rescue ($300 per thread), The Stack ($750 for up to four threads), and AI Ops ($350/month, cancel anytime).

Prices shown are the whole price for the described scope. If your job needs something outside that scope, we tell you the extra number before we touch anything, and you decide. No work starts, and no money is owed, until you approve a quote.

02 — quotes, payment, invoices

Every job starts with a quote — by email or phone, confirmed in writing. The quote is free. Nothing is owed until you say go.

We invoice by email. Invoices are due on receipt unless the invoice or your service page states a different term (monthly plans are billed at the start of each month). Work may pause on overdue invoices; we'll tell you before it does.

Monthly plans (care plan, submittal coordination, AI Ops) renew monthly until you cancel. Cancel anytime — email us before the next billing date and you won't be charged for the following month. No lock-in, no exit fees.

03 — revisions & changes

Each deliverable includes two (2) consolidated revision rounds. Corrections to match the agreed spec are always free. Work outside the agreed scope (new pages, extra trades, a second project hiding in one thread) is re-quoted before it proceeds — you'll never discover a surprise on an invoice.

04 — cancellation & refunds

Before work starts: cancel a quoted job any time at no cost.

After work starts: fixed-price jobs are refundable pro rata for work not yet performed. If we've delivered the deliverable and you simply don't want it, we'll discuss it like adults — but the work performed is owed.

Monthly plans: cancel anytime; the current month is not refunded, and nothing bills after that.

If we're late or get it wrong: if we miss an agreed turnaround through our own fault and can't make it right in time for your need, you don't pay for that deliverable. That's the fair version.

We work from what you give us. You're responsible for:

  • Accuracy of what you provide — plans, project documents, account credentials, business details, and the content you want published. We mark up what's issued; we can't verify facts you didn't give us.
  • Access and credentials — domain registrar, hosting, email accounts, project document portals, or the AI session exports relevant to your job. Share only what's needed; see the Privacy Policy for how we handle it.
  • Right to use the material — you confirm you have the rights to documents, plans, images, and session logs you send us (for construction work: you're entitled to use the issued set for your project).

For construction work specifically: quantities and estimates support your bid — final pricing, means and methods, and field verification remain with you.
