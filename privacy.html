privacy policy — short, because there isn't much to say

Your data stays yours. Here's exactly what that means.

This site collects almost nothing, and your project data is handled like the confidential work product it is. This page says what we hold, why, where it lives, and how to get it deleted.

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Operated by Richmond Park Group LLC, Florida, USA
01 — what we collect

Only what you hand us to do your job:

  • Contact information — your name, business name, email address, phone number if you offer it, and anything you tell us in the course of hiring us.
  • Project materials — the files you send: plans and drawing sets, brand assets, content, website or email account details, invoices and correspondence.
  • AI session data (AI lane) — the ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini threads, exports, logs, or links you share so we can finish the work.
  • Billing records — what was quoted, invoiced, and paid. We don't take card-on-file at signup; payments happen against invoices.
02 — what we don't collect
The short version No accounts, no logins, no analytics, no tracking cookies, no advertising pixels, no profiling, no data brokers. This site is static pages on Cloudflare. We can't show you a cookie banner because there's nothing to banner.

This website sets no cookies and deploys no analytics. If we ever add measurement tooling, this page changes first — and it will say exactly what was added.

03 — why we have it & where it lives

We use your data for two things: doing the work you hired us for and billing you for it. That's it.

It lives in two places:

  • Email — our correspondence with you, on self-hosted mail infrastructure under our control.
  • Project working storage — the files and session exports for your job, on storage we control, kept only as long as the project needs.

We use a small number of service providers as infrastructure, not as data customers: Cloudflare (this website) and the payment rails your invoice travels on. They process what they must to function; none of them get your project data because of us. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with anyone — not for marketing, not for "partners," not ever.

04 — your ai session data

If you share AI-agent sessions with us, this is the deal, and it's unconditional:

  • Confidential. Your threads are treated as your confidential material. We read them to do your job and for nothing else.
  • Never training data. Your sessions are not used to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or improve any AI model — ours or anyone else's.
  • Never resold, never shared. Not with vendors, not with "AI partners," not with anyone.
  • Deleted on request. Ask, and your session data is deleted — including from working storage and mail archives — and you get written confirmation.

Practical note from our side: when your work requires passing material through AI tools, we use accounts and configurations that don't contribute your content to provider training. If your thread contains secrets (API keys, credentials), you can strip them before sending — or tell us and we'll treat them as rotate-after-use.

05 — social publishing

We run our own content-scheduling tools on infrastructure we control, and one of them connects to social platforms — including TikTok — to publish posts to our own accounts, on our own behalf.

  • Our accounts only. The integration touches accounts we own and authorize ourselves. It never posts to, or reads, anyone else's.
  • Tokens stay on our hardware. Platform credentials live on our self-hosted server — not on someone else's cloud.
  • Nothing shared, nothing sold. No analytics brokers, no "marketing partners." Platform data stays where it lands.
  • Disconnect = delete. Revoke the connection in the platform's settings — or email us — and the stored token is deleted from our server.
06 — how long we keep it

We keep project data while your project is active, then for a 90-day archive window after completion — long enough to handle a "wait, where did that file go?" — after which working files and session data are deleted. Invoices and billing records are kept as long as tax law requires us to keep them, which is longer than we'd otherwise choose.

Want it gone sooner? See below — you don't have to wait out the window.

07 — deletion & your questions

Email legal@heynewell.com from the address we know you by and say the word. We'll delete the data you name — including session logs — and confirm in writing. Ask what we hold and we'll tell you plainly. Ask for a copy of your own project files and we'll send them.

If you have a privacy question or complaint that email doesn't resolve, say so in the same thread and a human (Jordan) answers it — there's no form to chase and no ticket queue.

08 — changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version goes live on this page with a new date. Changes that would affect how we handle existing client data get announced to those clients directly — we won't quietly change the deal on data we're already holding. This policy is part of our Terms of Service.
